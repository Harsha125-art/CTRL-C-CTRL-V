BitNBuild Hackathon Presentation

HireRank — Autonomous Technical Interview & Verification Platform

Judge Pitch, Architecture Walkthrough & Live Demo Guide

1. The 30-Second Elevator Pitch

"Traditional technical hiring is broken: resume screening is flooded with AI buzzwords, while human technical interview loops consume hundreds of engineering hours. On the other hand, automated coding quizzes fail to test real-world architectural thinking, communication, or code reasoning.

HireRank is an autonomous technical interview simulator that unifies batch resume ingestion, client-side Computer Vision integrity tracking, concurrent coding in Monaco, dynamic adaptive question branching, and Evidence-First Scoring where every rating is backed by exact verbatim quotes from the candidate transcript."

2. End-to-End System Workflow

[Recruiter Batch Ingestion]
  -> Multi-PDF Resume Upload (pdf2json + AI extraction of Name, Email, Skills)
  -> Customize Target Job Description & Role Context
  -> Automated Email Dispatch (HTML Invite + Unique Token + Mailto Fallback)

[Candidate Live Assessment]
  -> Opens Email Invite (/ ?invite=inv-xxx)
  -> Reviews Resume-to-Reality Claims (3 Key Verification Topics)
  -> Split-Screen Chamber:
       • Client-Side MediaPipe Edge Face Tracking (Centering & Multi-face review markers)
       • Explain-While-You-Build (Monaco IDE Python/JS + live speech transcription)
       • Adaptive Question Engine (Dynamic branching off candidate spoken words)

[Verified Output & Triage]
  -> Evidence-First Evaluator (Verbatim quotes + exact timestamps)
  -> Candidate Growth Coach (Personalized study roadmap)
  -> Recruiter Audit Dossier (Clickable transcript jumps & fast-track triage)

3. 2-Minute Live Demo Script for Judges

Act 1: Initial Role Gate (15s)

Open http://localhost:3000. Point out the clean initial role gate that separates Recruiter and Candidate portals. Click "1-Click Recruiter Demo (Sarah Jenkins)".

Act 2: Batch Resume Upload & Email Dispatch (35s)

Switch to the "Batch Upload & Invite" tab. Drop 2 or 3 PDF resumes. Demonstrate how HireRank automatically extracts candidate names, emails, and technical skills into an interactive queue. Click "Send Email Invitations to All" to trigger automated email dispatch and link generation.

Act 3: Candidate Adaptive Chamber (40s)

Switch to candidate mode. Open "Interview Invitations" to see the pending assessment. Click "Accept & Enter Interview Chamber". Show the Resume-to-Reality claims review, launch the live split-screen chamber with MediaPipe face tracking, the Monaco Editor workspace, and the non-repeating adaptive question engine.

Act 4: Evidence-First Scoring & Recruiter Triage (30s)

Conclude the session. Show the candidate's Growth Coach roadmap. Switch back to Recruiter view and open the candidate's Audit Dossier: demonstrate clicking CV markers to jump to transcript seconds, and how every rubric score quotes the candidate verbatim.

4. Key Differentiators to Emphasize

Dimension Conventional Approach HireRank Innovation
Proctoring Aggressive auto-disqualification on false positives. Non-disqualifying Review Markers: Edge CV logs timestamped markers on the recruiter timeline for human review.
Scoring Opaque numerical score or shallow AI summaries. Evidence-First Scoring: Every rubric score is backed by an exact verbatim quote from the transcript.
Adaptive Probes Static LeetCode questions easily memorized. Dynamic Branching Engine: Questions probe project claims and branch off spoken answers with anti-repetition guards.
Recruiter Pipeline Fragmented spreadsheets and manual email outreach. Integrated Batch Engine: Multi-resume upload, automated extraction, and 1-click email invitation links.
Candidate Value Generic rejection emails with zero feedback. Candidate Growth Coach: Concrete study roadmap derived from detected technical gaps.

5. Anticipated Judge Q&A

Q: Why run computer vision on the client side?
A: Running MediaPipe Face Mesh in the browser eliminates server GPU costs, bandwidth streaming bottlenecks, and ensures candidate privacy since raw camera frames never leave the client's machine.

Q: How do you prevent AI hallucination during candidate grading?
A: The Evidence-First Scoring prompt requires exact verbatim quotes and timestamps from the transcript. If an evaluation criterion lacks exact quote verification, high ratings cannot be awarded.
